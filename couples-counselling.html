Couples Counselling

Couples counselling in Sheffield and online, informed by Imago Relationship Therapy techniques and attachment theory. A safe, structured space where each of you can feel heard. Robert Highfield, MNCPS (Accred).

Does this sound familiar?

You care about each other, but somehow you keep having the same argument. Or a small disagreement ends with one of you shutting down and the other feeling unheard.

It is rarely really about the dishes, the phone or the money. Underneath, it is usually about feeling safe, valued and close, and about old patterns we each bring from earlier relationships.

Left alone, distance can grow quietly, until you feel more like housemates, or even opponents.

Couples counselling gives you both a calmer, more structured way to talk and listen, so you can understand what is really going on and find your way back to each other.

How it works

1. A free 50-minute session

We start with a relaxed trial session, so you can experience how I work and see whether you would like to work with me. There is no obligation to continue.

2. Sessions of 75 minutes

If you would like to continue, we meet for 75 minutes at a time. I guide you step by step through a structured way of speaking and listening, so each of you feels heard.

3. Practising together

You will have simple practices to try at home between sessions, so what you learn becomes part of everyday life together.

My approach

I use techniques from Imago Relationship Therapy, alongside what we know about attachment: how our earliest relationships shape what we need and what we fear in close ones.

In sessions, I guide a structured dialogue. One of you speaks while the other listens, mirrors back what they have heard and checks they have understood. This slows things down, takes the heat out of old arguments and helps you see each other with more curiosity.

I do not take sides. My job is to hold a steady, safe space so that you can both be heard.

Is couples counselling right for us?

Couples counselling works best when you both want to be there and both feel safe with each other.

It is not suitable if there is current violence, abuse or controlling behaviour in the relationship, or if one of you is afraid of the other. If that is your situation, individual support is the safer place to start. The National Domestic Abuse Helpline, run by Refuge, is free to call on 0808 2000 247, 24 hours a day.

Common questions

Do we both need to come?

Yes. Sessions are for the two of you together, and the approach works best when you both want to be there.

What if we are not sure the relationship will last?

You do not need to be certain. Some couples come to repair things, some to understand what is happening, and some to work out what they want. I do not take sides or push you towards any particular outcome.

Are you a certified Imago therapist?

Not yet. I have trained in Imago Relationship Therapy and use its techniques, alongside attachment-informed counselling. I am happy to answer any questions about my training.

What if we want to stop part-way through a block?

That is fine. Sessions you have attended are charged at £75 each, and I refund the rest of the block.

What if we need to cancel or rearrange a session?

Please give at least 72 hours' notice. With less notice, the session fee will be charged.

Can we meet online?

Yes. You will need a private space, a stable internet connection, and a device that lets us see each other. Many couples find it easiest to sit together at one device.

Is it confidential?

What you tell me stays between us, with the usual exceptions where someone is at serious risk of harm or the law requires me to share information. I will go through this with you in detail at the start.
