About Me

Types of Therapy

I can help with a range of issues, including:

Mood and anxiety

  • Anxiety, worry and panic
  • Low mood and depression
  • Stress and burnout

Loss, trauma and difficult experiences

  • Grief, bereavement and loss
  • Trauma, including the after-effects of abuse or exploitation
  • Feeling overwhelmed, low or hopeless

Sense of self and relationships

  • Low self-esteem and confidence
  • Relationship difficulties (working with you as an individual)
  • Loneliness and isolation
  • Anger and other difficult emotions
  • Patterns from childhood or the past that affect you now

Life, work and direction

  • Significant life changes and transitions
  • Work, career or study-related pressures
  • Questions of identity, meaning and purpose
